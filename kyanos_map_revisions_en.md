## 4. Design & Typography Notes for Manual Revision
1. **Fonts**: Use modern serif or futuristic geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Cinzel*, *Montserrat*, *Orbitron*, *Eurostile*).
2. **Effects**: Add a subtle outer cyan/teal glow (`#00f0ff`, blur: 4px–8px) to all holographic labels.
3. **Contrast**: Ensure text placed above ocean waters has a dark translucent backdrop badge (`rgba(1, 12, 28, 0.75)`) with a thin 1px cyan border.
