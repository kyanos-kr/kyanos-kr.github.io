🌊 The Coastal Promenade of the Yellow Sea: Oido Island (오이도 / 烏耳島), Siheung-si, Gyeonggi-do
🦀 Open-Air Embankment Fish Bazaars, Steaming Roasted Clam Boulevards & The Solitary Red Lighthouse

1. An Island That Is No Longer an Island: The Soul of Oido

To international travelers, Korea’s West Coast offers a dramatic interplay between land and ocean. Among these coastal treasures, Oido (오이도) holds a distinctive place. Though its historical name literally translates to “Crow’s Ear Island” owing to its topographical shape on antique nautical charts, land reclamation projects long ago connected this ancient islet directly to the mainland. Yet, locals and visitors alike steadfastly continue to call it an island. It is, in every emotional and sensory aspect, an island of the mind—a place where the endless horizon of the Yellow Sea breathes fresh life into weary city dwellers.

Connected southward to Daebudo Island via the monumental Sihwa Seawall, Oido has evolved into a vibrant Marine Tourism District. Along the lengthy sea barrier and coastal fencing, pedestrian promenades stretch toward the sunset, accompanied by the gentle clinking of fishing tackle and the calls of hovering seagulls.

🐟 Living Marine Bazaar: Small parasols lined along the seawall where local fishwives display their daily sea bounty / Photo by Phaeton

2. The Symphony of Open-Air Fishermen Stalls

What truly enchants the wanderer walking atop the Oido embankment is the spontaneous open-air fish market (Nanjeon). Underneath an endless succession of colorful parasols fluttering in the ocean wind, local fishing vendors set up large plastic basins brimming with live sea creatures freshly brought in by the working wooden boats in the offing.

Their weathered hands work with seasoned agility, scaling, filleting, and packing seafood as lively bartering echoes through the salt air. In springtime and early summer, Ganjaemi (young skate / ray) is in its prime, sliced fresh for visitors. Near the late season, winter oysters and glistening razor clams (matjoga) are sold at affordable, honest market prices. It is authentic, unfiltered coastal living that modern urban arcades can never replicate.

⚓ Rhythms of the Tides: Coastal fishing skiffs preparing for their next voyage upon the expansive waters / Photo by Phaeton

3. Culinary Feast: From Sliced Raw Fish to Roasted Clam Boulevards

Oido is famed across the Seoul Metropolitan Area as the definitive seafood destination. Travelers can choose their dining experience according to their personal style:

  • The Fishermen’s Auction Board (어판장 & Cho-jang Restaurants): For an authentic, highly economical feast, visitors purchase freshly filleted raw fish (Hoe) directly at the ground-floor stalls and head upstairs. With generous side delicacies like sea squirt (meongge) and spoon worms (gaebul) accompanying crisp lettuce wraps and spicy fish stew (Maeuntang), it provides unparalleled coastal bounty.
  • The Romantic Embankment Picnic: Many visitors purchase their fresh seafood directly from the outdoor parasol vendors and spread out a small mat right at the edge of the seawall pier. Watching mature couples and young sweethearts savoring seafood while looking out over the water reveals an unmistakable Korean romance.
  • The Multi-Story Seafood Promenade: Along the main boulevard, lined with towering seafood houses, sizzling platters of roasted shell clams (Jogae-gui) and rich seafood noodle soup (Bajirak Kalguksu) fill the air with irresistible aromas.

🚇 Step-by-Step Transit Instructions from Central Seoul

  1. Board Seoul Subway Line 4 (Blue Line) bound for Oido (오이도) from major stations like Seoul Station, Myeongdong, or Dongdaemun. Ride comfortably all the way to the terminal station, Oido Station (approx. 70 mins from Seoul Station).
  2. Exit via Exit 2. Cross over to the bus transfer plaza right in front of the station.
  3. Take Bus No. 30-2 or City Bus 11-C. Many fellow passengers wearing comfortable windbreakers and sunhats will be heading in the exact same direction—simply follow the flow of travelers. If ever in doubt, politely show the Korean address above to the friendly driver.
  4. Alight at the Oido Marine Complex / Red Lighthouse (오이도해양단지·빨강등대) stop. The refreshing sea wind and coastal embankment await just steps away!
