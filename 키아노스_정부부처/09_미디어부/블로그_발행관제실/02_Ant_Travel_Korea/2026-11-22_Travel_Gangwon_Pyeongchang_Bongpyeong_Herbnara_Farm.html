Tucked deep inside the serene folds of Bongpyeong, Pyeongchang, along the celebrated waters of Heungjeong Valley, lies Herbnara Farm (허브나라농원). Founded in 1993 as South Korea’s very first tourist farm dedicated entirely to herbs, this green haven sits at the ideal human biometric altitude of 700 meters above sea level. Here, cool clean mountain breezes and mineral-rich streams create the perfect microclimate for over 100 varieties of aromatic herbs and medicinal mountain flora to flourish in complete ecological harmony.

Recognized in 2009 by the Korean Ministry of Environment as one of the country's top ten eco-tourism destinations, Herbnara has evolved from a quiet agricultural grove into a premier wellness retreat. For international travelers journeying beyond Seoul into the mountainous heart of Gangwon State, it offers a peaceful sensory counterpoint to modern urban bustle.

Why Visit Herbnara Farm?

  • 10 Immersive Thematic Sanctuaries: Wander through the colorful Palette Garden, the literary Shakespeare Garden, the vibrant Butterfly Garden, and tranquil forest trails beneath towering pines and birch trees.
  • Year-Round Glasshouse Conservatory: Even when alpine autumn gives way to winter snowfall, the indoor glasshouses brim with blooming chamomile, scented geraniums, and tropical botanicals.
  • Wholesome Farm-to-Table Gastronomy: Taste the signature Herb Bibimbap at Birch House Restaurant, garnished with crisp freshly plucked organic herbs, followed by warm lavender tea at the Garden Cafe.
  • Interactive Sensory Workshops: Handcraft your own souvenir aroma perfumes, organic herbal soaps, and decorative jelly candles at the Herb Craft Studio.

Getting There: Step-by-Step Route

By KTX High-Speed Train & Public Transit

  1. KTX Gangneung Line: Board the KTX at Seoul Station or Cheongnyangni Station and alight at Pyeongchang Station (평창역) (approx. 1 hour 25 minutes).
  2. Local Bus / Taxi: From Pyeongchang Station, take a local bus bound for Bongpyeong Bus Terminal (approx. 20 min), then take a brief taxi ride (approx. 10 min) along the Heungjeong Valley road directly to Herbnara Farm.

By Rental Car

From Seoul, take the Yeongdong Expressway (Route 50) eastward, exit at Myeonon IC (면온IC) or Pyeongchang IC (평창IC), and follow the scenic mountain road northward along Heungjeong Valley. Spacious on-site parking is available free of charge for visitors.

Insider Tips & Best Photo Spots

  • The Palette Garden Bridge: Capture the sweeping view of colorful flowerbeds framing the crystal stream of Heungjeong Valley. In the late afternoon, golden sunlight creates magical natural backlighting.
  • Local Culinary Harmony: Combine your visit with Bongpyeong’s regional specialty—traditional Memil Makguksu (chilled buckwheat noodles) and buckwheat pancakes (Memiljeon) at nearby village eateries.
  • Garden Etiquette: Please stay on designated cobblestone and wooden pathways to protect fragile root systems. Outside food, picnic mats, and pets over small breed size are restricted to preserve the ecological tranquility.

A Philosopher's Note

Modern life frequently traps the human spirit in relentless acceleration and digital abstraction. Yet, sitting quietly upon a weathered cedar bench at Herbnara with the soft murmur of Heungjeong Valley in the background, one rediscovers the ancient wisdom of the earth. Here, growth is never hurried; every mint leaf, blossom, and pine needle unfurls in its own ordained season. In breathing this gentle alpine fragrance, the soul quietly remembers how to be whole.
