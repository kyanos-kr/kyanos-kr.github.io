🌊 Coastal Escape on the Outskirts of Seoul: Daemyung Port (대명포구) in Gimpo, Gyeonggi-do
🐟 An Authentic Inbound Guide to Bustling Fish Markets, Salted Fermented Seafood, and Scenic Straits of Chojidaegyo

▲ Coastal Haven of Gimpo: Peaceful daytime waters and docked fishing vessels at Daemyung Port / Photo by Paeton

Leaving the bustling metropolis behind on an early autumn morning, the journey along the Olympic Highway and through Gimpo (김포) runs parallel to the tranquil curves of the Han River. Nestled at the southwestern tip where the river meets the Yellow Sea lies Daemyung Port (대명항 / 대명포구)—Gimpo’s only registered national fishing port and a treasured sanctuary for those seeking raw, authentic maritime life without traveling hours away from Seoul.

Directly across the narrow Yeomha strait stands Ganghwa Island (강화도), linked in the distance by the grand arches of Choji Bridge (초지대교). The port retains a distinctive edge—historic security fences line portions of the coastline protecting coastal fisheries, lending the landscape a poignant, reflective atmosphere. Yet beyond the perimeter, the sea air hums with the vitality of local fishermen returning with the day's fresh bounty.

▲ The Abundant Catch: Rows of authentic seafood stalls offering live flounder, gizzard shad, and seasonal shellfish / Photo by Paeton

1. Direct-from-the-Boat Seafood Market: Freshness at Honest Prices

The pulsating heart of Daemyung Port is its enclosed fish market, where fishing families sell their catches directly to travelers and locals alike. Unlike the towering commercial distributors in downtown Seoul, the prices here remain remarkably humble and grounded. Two long corridors of stalls showcase seasonal treasures: late autumn gizzard shad (전어), firm-fleshed blue crabs (꽃게), and glistening flounder (도다리).

With the traditional kimchi-making season (Kimjang) fast approaching, Daemyung is also renowned throughout the Gyeonggi province for its rich, naturally cured salted shrimp (새우젓) and fermented fish pastes (액젓). The crisp aroma of salt and sea gives travelers an unvarnished glimpse into Korea’s ancestral foodways.

▲ Leisurely Moments: The coastal park pavilion where visitors enjoy freshly prepared raw fish / Photo by Paeton

2. A Dish of Flounder and the Poetry of the Autumn Chills

One of the greatest pleasures at Daemyung Port is simplicity itself. Visitors can purchase a generous plate of freshly sliced live flounder (도다리회) prepared on the spot for an astonishingly modest price (just 10,000 KRW). Stepping over to the neighboring coastal park and resting beneath a traditional wooden pavilion, one can open a modest bottle of local beverage and savor the chewy, sweet freshness of the ocean.

Even as the brisk late-autumn wind whispers through one's coat, the quiet serenity of the waterfront keeps intoxication at bay, leaving only a pure, contemplative calm. Nearby, children from local schools visit the adjacent Marine Park and outdoor exhibits, bringing cheerful laughter that warms the cool sea air.

▲ The Unhurried Tide: Fishing boats resting at low tide along the mudflats of the Yeomha straits / Photo by Paeton

3. Essential Inbound Traveler's Guide to Daemyung Port

  • Location: Daemyung Port (대명항), Daemyung-ri, Daegot-myeon, Gimpo-si, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea
  • How to Get There: Board Bus 60-3 from Yeongdeungpo Station (Line 1) or Songjeong Station (Line 5), which takes you directly to the Daemyung Port terminus. Alternatively, drive along Olympic Boulevard into Gimpo-daero (approx. 60–75 minutes from central Seoul).
  • Nearby Highlights: Gimpo Marine Park (함상공원 - featuring a retired naval destroyer LST-671), Deokpojin Historic Fortress (덕포진), and crossing over Choji Bridge to southern Ganghwa Island.
  • Must-Eat Delicacies: Seasonal sliced flounder (도다리회), king prawn salt bake (대하소금구이), spicy fish stew (매운탕), and deep-fried seafood sold by the port entrances.

For international travelers yearning for the genuine cadence of Korean seaside life—where fishermen mend their nets, tidal mudflats stretch into misty horizons, and fresh sashimi is shared in open pavilions—Daemyung Port offers an authentic, soulful journey that lingers in the heart long after the journey home.
