🍁 The Crown Jewel of Southern Seoraksan: Osaek Jujeongol Valley (오색 주전골, Yangyang)
🌊 From Ancient Minting Legends to the Emerald Depths of Yongso Cascade & Osaek Carbonated Springs

🍂 The Symphony of Crimson & Granite: A breathtaking view of Jujeongol Valley engulfed in peak autumn colors / Photo by Paeton

Nestled in the southern slopes of Mount Seorak (설악산, Seoraksan National Park)—Korea's most celebrated alpine biosphere reserve—lies Jujeongol Valley (주전골) in Yangyang County, Gangwon Province. While Mount Seorak is renowned for its sheer, razor-sharp peaks and grueling alpine ridges, Jujeongol offers a sublime counterpoint: an accessible, gently sloping paradise where towering granite cathedrals, emerald river basins, and fiery maples harmonize in effortless grandeur.

Renowned among seasoned mountaineers and world travelers as the quintessential autumn trek of the Korean Peninsula, Jujeongol features impeccably designed eco-decks and flat wooden promenades. Without requiring strenuous mountaineering gear, visitors of all ages can immerse themselves directly into the beating heart of Seorak’s primeval wilderness.

1. The Legend of Counterfeit Coins & The Healing Spring of Osaek

The intriguing name Jujeon (鑄錢, meaning "minting coins") is steeped in Joseon Dynasty folklore. Deep within the inaccessible caverns of this rugged valley, a group of bandits once hid away to forge counterfeit brass coins (Sangpyeong Tongbo). Discovered by the provincial governor's magistrate, the bandits were dispersed, and the mysterious gorge forever earned the moniker Jujeongol.

The journey begins at Osaek Mineral Spring (오색약수), discovered around 1500 AD by a Buddhist monk from Seongguksa Temple. Emerging naturally from a solid granite riverbed, the water is naturally carbonated and rich in iron, renowned for centuries for soothing digestive ailments and revitalizing fatigued travelers. Taking a sip of this effervescent, metallic spring water before beginning the trail is a timeless Korean ritual.

2. Yongso Cascade & The Dragon's Mythic Awakening

As hikers trace the gentle wooden trail along the whispering stream, the crescendo of the journey reveals itself at Yongso Waterfall (용소폭포). Here, pure alpine currents surge over smooth granite steps, plunging into an impossibly clear, 7-meter-deep emerald abyss.

According to regional mythology, two mythological serpents (Imoogi) spent a thousand years in this basin striving to attain dragonhood. When the fateful day of ascension arrived, one succeeded in rising gracefully into the heavenly clouds, while the other failed to break free, transforming into the petrified cliff that still shelters the falls today. The visual drama of the turquoise water against crimson maples makes Yongso one of the most photographed vistas in all of Gangwon.

3. Essential Practical Tips for International Travelers

  • Access from Seoul: Take an intercity express bus from Seoul Express Bus Terminal (Gangnam) or Dong Seoul Terminal to Osaek (오색) or Yangyang Terminal (approx. 2 hours 10 mins). Local buses frequently connect Yangyang Terminal to Osaek.
  • Trail Difficulty & Duration: The standard course runs from Osaek Mineral Spring Information Center to Yongso Waterfall (approx. 3.2 km one-way, 1 to 1.5 hours of relaxed walking). The trail is largely barrier-free with low incline, perfect for beginners and families.
  • Wellness & Hot Springs: After the autumn hike, relax at the nearby Osaek Hot Spring District, renowned for natural carbonated hot water springs that warm the core and rejuvenate weary muscles.
  • Local Mountain Cuisine: Indulge in authentic Wild Vegetable Bibimbap (Sanchae Bibimbap) served with fragrant deodeok root, seasoned mountain herbs, and acorn jelly (Dotorimuk) at the traditional dining village at the trail entrance.
