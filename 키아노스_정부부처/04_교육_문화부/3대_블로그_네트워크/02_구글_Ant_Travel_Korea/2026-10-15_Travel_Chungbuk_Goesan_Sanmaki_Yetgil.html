Nestled deep within the verdant heart of North Chungcheong Province, there exists an enchanting mountain trail where pristine waters, gentle hills, and lingering autumn foliage blend into an idyllic landscape painting. Sanmaki-Yetgil (산막이옛길) in Goesan is a restored 10-ri (approx. 4 kilometers) eco-walkway connecting Saorang Village to the secluded mountain hamlet of Sanmaki. Faithfully tracing the footsteps of ancestral villagers while hovering gently above the crystalline surface of Goesan Dam Lake on eco-friendly wooden decks, this tranquil pathway is rightfully celebrated as the crown jewel of inland Korean nature walks.

1. Heritage of Goesan Dam: Untouched Wilderness Preserved in Time

The profound ecological purity of Sanmaki-Yetgil owes its existence to a unique milestone in Korean modern history: Goesan Dam, completed in early 1957 as Korea's very first domestically engineered hydroelectric facility. Because human settlement and heavy industrialization were strictly restricted across the reservoir's catchment basin for decades, the surrounding hills and shores remained in a near-primeval state.

As travelers stroll along the deck, the emerald water curves gracefully against dramatic rocky bluffs. Dense canopies of Korean pine, royal azaleas, and majestic oaks provide a soothing embrace, filling the air with invigorating phytoncides that instantly wash away the tension of modern city life.

2. Poetic Reverie in the Autumn Rain: 26 Storied Wonders

Visiting Sanmaki-Yetgil on a rainy autumn day is not a disappointment, but an unexpected blessing. As steady droplets fall through the foliage and water vapor rises from the lake, the ridges reveal themselves like soft brush strokes on traditional Hanji paper. The earthly aroma of damp moss, pine needles, and crisp mountain soil awakens the senses.

Along this 4km stretch, travelers discover 26 distinct cultural and natural wonders. Highlights include:

  • Dolmen Rest Area (고인돌쉼터): An ancient stone resting spot honoring prehistoric heritage.
  • Yeon리지 (연리지 - Entwined Love Trees): Two trees harmoniously joined as one trunk, symbolizing enduring affection.
  • Pine Tree Suspension Bridge (소나무출렁다리): An exhilarating treetop crossing woven between sturdy pine limbs.
  • Noru Spring (노루샘) & Tiger's Cave (호랑이굴): Legendary sanctuaries where mountain roe deer drank and winter beasts sheltered.
  • Oriole Observatory (꾀꼬리전망대): A cantilevered glass pavilion perched high above the shimmering water.

3. Essential Travel Logistics & Practical Guide

Whether you seek a romantic couple's stroll, a heartwarming family excursion, or a solitary philosophical journey, Sanmaki-Yetgil offers comfortable trails suited for all walking abilities.

📍 International Traveler's Pocket Guide

  • Location: Sanmaki-Yetgil, Chilseong-myeon, Goesan-gun, Chungcheongbuk-do, South Korea (충북 괴산군 칠성면 사은리 산막이옛길)
  • Trail Length: Main Water Trail approx. 4 km (One-way walking time: 1.5 ~ 2 hours)
  • Ferry Option: Scenic passenger cruise boats operate regularly between the entrance dock and Sanmaki Village, allowing visitors to walk one way and cruise back across the open lake.
  • Hiking Courses: Course 1 (4.4 km via Noru Spring to Sanmaki Village, ~3 hrs) / Course 2 (2.9 km via Jindallae Hill, ~2 hrs)
  • Local Flavors: Rustic food stalls near the village serve warm acorn jelly (dotori-muk), wild herb bibimbap, and savory Korean pancakes paired with regional Goesan organic cider.
