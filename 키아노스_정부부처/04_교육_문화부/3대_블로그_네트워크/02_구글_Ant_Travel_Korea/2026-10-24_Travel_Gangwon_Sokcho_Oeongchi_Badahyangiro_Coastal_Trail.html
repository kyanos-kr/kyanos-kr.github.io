🌊 Sovereign Maritime Wonder: Oeongchi Badahyangiro (외옹치 바다향기로) in Sokcho, Gangwon-do
🚶‍♂️ An Exclusive Inbound Guide to Hidden Cliff Decks, Historic Security Wire Fences, Aromatic Pine Paths & Authentic Fishing Village Life

⚓ Gateway to the Deep Blue: The welcoming entrance of Badahyangiro ('The Sea Scent Trail') connecting Oeongchi Beach and Oeongchihang Port / Photo by Paeton

Across the Korean peninsula, travelers and nature lovers are passionately seeking beautifully preserved coastal walking trails. Yet among all coastal boardwalks along the Eastern Coastline, none carries a more dramatic history and raw oceanic majesty than Sokcho Oeongchi 'Badahyangiro' (속초 외옹치 바다향기로). Stretching gracefully along 1.5 kilometers of rugged coastal bluffs, this scenic footway links the tranquil sands of Oeongchi Beach with the rustic charm of Oeongchihang Port.

Following an armed infiltration incident in the late 1960s, this entire headland was completely sealed off behind barbed-wire military barriers for over six decades. Restricted from civilian footsteps for 65 continuous years, its ancient rocky promontories, dense black pine groves, and pristine emerald waters remained frozen in time. In recent years, an intensive 2.5-billion-won public-private project delicately built a sweeping wooden deck over the crashing surf, opening this secretive national sanctuary to the world.

1. Four Distinct Themes: From Bamboo Whispers to Security Memorials

Though compact at 1.5 kilometers, the Badahyangiro trail is masterfully partitioned into four captivating thematic sections: the Bamboo Path (대나무 명상길), the Sky Observatory Deck (하늘 전망대), the Security Exhibition Section (안보 체험길), and the Rock Cliff Promenade (암석 관찰길).

While neighboring Gangneung’s famous Jeongdongjin Bada Bukaegil has become an extensively commercialized, fee-charging attraction, Badahyangiro remains a remarkably welcoming, open-access public treasure. Walking here feels intimately grounded—the sea spray gently touches your face while the fragrance of aromatic coastal pine cones blends harmoniously with the rich saline air.

2. Whispering Pines and Living Relics of Cold War History

As you round the promontory, the wooden walkway rises gently into ancient pine forests. Here, centuries-old black pines have bent their trunks into dramatic serpentine shapes, shaped by fierce winter typhoons over generations.

Along the Security Memorial section, portions of the original rusty barbed-wire coastal barriers and guard posts have been deliberately preserved. Rather than feeling grim, colorful peace ribbons and art installations flutter against the steel netting, offering visitors a poignant space to reflect on the value of peace and how long-guarded military frontiers can evolve into bridges of human healing.

3. Arriving at Oeongchihang Port: Warm Fishing Village Hospitality

Exiting the southern tip of Badahyangiro reveals Oeongchihang Port (외옹치항), a delightfully compact, genuine working fishing harbor nestled between the rocky headlands. Unlike the sprawling commercial seafood centers of Daepo Port nearby, Oeongchi retains its humble village heartbeat.

Here, local fishermen mend their nylon squid nets under the autumn sun while friendly vendor grandmothers slice fresh catch—flatfish, rockfish, sea squirts, and seasonal squid—right on harborside decks. It is the perfect place to pause, savor a steaming bowl of spicy fish stew (Maeuntang), and strike up warm, unhurried conversations with the local seafaring folk.

🗺️ Badahyangiro Essential Visitor's Field Guide

  • 📍 Trailhead Locations:
    • North Gate: Oeongchi Beach Trailhead (Gangwon-do, Sokcho-si, Daepo-dong 656-14)
    • South Gate: Oeongchihang Port Trailhead (Gangwon-do, Sokcho-si, Daepo-dong 728)
  • 🕒 Operating Hours:
    • Summer (Apr–Oct): 06:00 – 20:00 (Gates close at 19:30)
    • Winter (Nov–Mar): 07:00 – 18:00 (Gates close at 17:30)
  • 🎫 Admission & Parking: 100% Free. Free public parking available at both Oeongchi Beach and Oeongchihang Port.
  • 🚶 Course Distance & Duration: Approx. 1.5 km (40–50 minutes at an unhurried, photography-friendly stroll).
  • 🚌 Public Transport from Seoul: Express Bus from Seoul Express Bus Terminal / Dong Seoul to Sokcho Express Bus Terminal (approx. 2 hrs 10 mins). From Sokcho Terminal, a quick 5-minute taxi (approx. 4,500 KRW) or city bus #7 / #9 brings you directly to Oeongchi Beach.
  • 💡 Ant's Inbound Insider Tip: Start your walk at Oeongchi Beach in the late afternoon (around 16:30), enjoy the golden hour glow reflecting off the rocky cliffs, and arrive at Oeongchihang Port just in time for an authentic raw fish dinner overlooking the evening harbor lights!
