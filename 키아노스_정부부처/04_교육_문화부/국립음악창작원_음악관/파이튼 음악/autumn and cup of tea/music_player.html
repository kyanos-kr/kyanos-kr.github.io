Autumn and a Cup of Tea

Autumn seeps into my heart once again.
The fury of a colossal furnace,
The fate of the crimson twilight,
Spills its icy flames upon the earth.

As my breath drifts over the river
On this land where warmth has vanished,
The cold air sends shivers through my body,
Yet, I find solace in a cup of tea at a café.

Stretching my arms wide in the swaying reed fields,
I embrace the season.
The gentle breeze brushes against my cheeks,
Carrying her fragrant scent,
Whispering autumn’s sweet temptation.

Love and hatred alike,
Once burned fiercely in the fire of youth,
Now turn to ashen leaves,
Falling as a weary traveler upon the earth.
